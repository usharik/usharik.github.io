MySongBook – Tabs & Chords

Guitar Pro tab player, interactive fretboard, lyrics with chords & private cloud sync.

GET IT ONGoogle Play

MySongBook is a companion app for guitarists, bassists and singers. Learn songs from Guitar Pro tabs with a built-in offline synthesizer, follow the notes on a playable virtual fretboard, and keep your lyrics-with-chords songbook in one place — with instant transposition and chord diagrams. Everything works offline; an optional backup to your own Google Drive keeps the library in sync between phone and tablet.

  • 🎸 Guitar Pro tab player

    Plays .gp, .gp3, .gp4, .gp5 and .gpx files with crisp notation, multi-track mixing, variable tempo (0.25×–2×), metronome and section looping.

  • 🎵 Interactive fretboard

    Real-time note positions and fingerings synced with playback. Tap any fret to hear the note; Player or Audience view; custom tunings incl. 7-string and bass.

  • 📝 Lyrics & chords songbook

    Clean chord sheets, one-tap transposition, capo setting and interactive chord diagrams with alternative voicings.

  • ☁️ Private, offline-first sync

    Optional backup to a hidden folder in your own Google Drive or to a local folder. No account with us, no third-party servers.
