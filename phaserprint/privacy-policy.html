Phaser Print – Privacy Policy

Application: Phaser Print (Android, package ru.usharik.phaserprint)
Developer: Alexey Usharik (“we”, “us”)
Effective date: 18 September 2026 · Last updated: 18 September 2026

Summary
  • Phaser Print processes selected documents locally on your Android device and sends them only to the printer you choose.
  • The app does not operate its own server, show advertising, create user accounts, or upload documents.
  • With the Firebase configuration included in a release, Firebase Crashlytics receives crash reports and technical error diagnostics. Diagnostics may include the printer name and model involved in the operation, but not document contents or the printer's network address.
  • Saved printer names and network addresses are stored locally on your device and can be removed in the app.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how the Phaser Print Android application (the “App”) handles information. The App is an unofficial third-party printing utility and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Xerox Corporation.

2. Information the App handles

2.1 Documents selected for printing

When you choose a PDF or image using the Android system document picker, the App reads that document to render the selected pages and prepare a print job. The document is processed on your device and is not uploaded to the developer or to an analytics service.

2.2 Saved printer information

The App stores the printer name, local network address and TCP port of printers that you explicitly add. This information is used to show your saved printers and to send print jobs to the printer you select.

2.3 Information we do not collect

  • No account information, passwords or payment information.
  • No advertising ID, Android ID, IMEI or other device identifiers are intentionally read by the App.
  • No precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS or call data.
  • No Firebase Analytics, advertising, behavioral profiling or usage analytics.
  • No user documents, document contents or printer network addresses are sent to the developer or to Firebase.

3. Printing and local network transfer

When you explicitly start a print job, the App sends the selected document’s rendered print data to the selected printer over your local Wi‑Fi network. Compatible older Xerox Phaser models use JetDirect/QPDL over TCP port 9100. This is a user-initiated transfer to the destination you selected, not a transfer to the developer.

Some older printers do not support encrypted transport for this protocol. Use the App on a trusted Wi‑Fi network and avoid sending confidential documents over networks you do not control.

4. Permissions and system components

The App uses network access to discover printers and send print jobs. It uses the Android system document picker, so it does not request broad access to all files on your device. The Android Print Service component exposes saved printers to compatible apps only after you enable the service in Android settings.

The App does not create user accounts. Android removes the App’s private data when you uninstall the App.

6. Third-party services and links

6.1 Firebase Crashlytics

Release builds may use Firebase Crashlytics, a crash-reporting service provided by Google Firebase. When enabled, Crashlytics may receive crash reports, stack traces, app version, operating-system and device information, an installation identifier and other technical diagnostics. The App adds limited diagnostic context: the operation being performed and the name and model of the printer involved. It does not add document contents, document names or printer network addresses to Crashlytics.

Crashlytics is used only to find crashes and technical errors and improve compatibility. It is not used for advertising or behavioral analytics. Google processes this information according to the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy. Crashlytics collection can be controlled by the App's release configuration; debug builds do not send Crashlytics reports.

6.2 External links

If you open an external link, your browser or the selected external app’s privacy policy applies.

7. Security

Documents are processed locally and are sent only after your explicit print action. Network security also depends on the Wi‑Fi network and printer protocol you use; no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience utility and is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App changes or when required by law. The current version is always available at https://usharik.github.io/phaserprint/privacy-policy.html; the “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.
